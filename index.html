Privacy Policy

WHOOP Uzbek AI

Personal wellness-information tool

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the intended data practices of WHOOP Uzbek AI (the “App”), a personal tool being developed to help an authorized user understand their WHOOP information in Uzbek and receive reports through Telegram. The App is in development. Data processing will begin only when the relevant features are deployed and the user authorizes access.

Health information: WHOOP data can reveal sensitive details about a person’s health and routines. The App is intended for the account holder’s personal use. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

1. Information the App May Process

After the user authorizes access through WHOOP OAuth, the App may retrieve the categories covered by the permissions the user grants:

  • Recovery metrics, including recovery score, heart-rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate, where available.
  • Sleep data, including duration, timing, and available sleep metrics.
  • Physiological cycle information and daily strain.
  • Workout details, such as activity type, duration, and available strain metrics.
  • Profile and body measurements, if the user grants the relevant permissions.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, such as authorization tokens, synchronization timestamps, and diagnostic logs.

The exact fields available depend on WHOOP’s API, the permissions granted, and the features enabled. The App does not claim to retrieve data that WHOOP does not make available through its API.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is intended to be used to synchronize authorized WHOOP data, calculate historical trends, compare recent measurements with the user’s own baseline, generate Uzbek-language explanations, deliver requested Telegram reports, maintain security, and troubleshoot failures. The App is not intended to sell personal information or use WHOOP data for advertising.

3. AI-Assisted Analysis

When AI analysis is enabled, selected metrics or summaries may be sent to the OpenAI API to generate explanations in Uzbek. The design aims to send only information needed for the requested analysis and to avoid sending direct identifiers when they are not required. OpenAI’s applicable terms and privacy documentation also apply to data processed by its service. The operator should review the current service terms before launch and update this notice before changing the AI provider or material data practices.

AI-generated content may be incomplete or incorrect and is informational only. It must not be treated as medical advice or as a substitute for professional care.

4. Telegram Reports

If Telegram delivery is enabled, the App sends the requested report to the Telegram account or chat configured by the user. Telegram processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy. The user should keep the bot and report chat private and should not forward personal wellness reports to people who are not authorized to see them.

5. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

The planned service stores the minimum data needed for historical analysis on its application server. The planned retention period for synchronized WHOOP records and generated reports is up to 90 days, after which they should be deleted automatically unless the user requests earlier deletion. If implementation or operational needs require a different retention period, this policy must be updated before that change takes effect.

OAuth tokens and application secrets must be stored separately from public code, protected with appropriate access controls, and never included in Telegram reports or public repositories. Reasonable safeguards will be used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Revoking WHOOP authorization stops future access when the revocation is recognized, but does not by itself delete information already stored by the App or by third-party services. The user may request deletion of App-held records by contacting the operator below.

6. Service Providers and Sharing

Depending on which features are enabled, data may be processed by WHOOP (to provide the API), OpenAI (for AI-assisted analysis), Telegram (to deliver reports), and the hosting provider used to operate the App. Those providers process information under their respective terms and policies. The operator does not intend to sell WHOOP data or share it for third-party advertising.

7. User Choices

The user may choose which WHOOP permissions to grant, stop using Telegram reports, revoke the App’s WHOOP authorization through the available WHOOP account controls, and request deletion of App-held data. Some features will not work if the permissions they require are not granted.

8. Children

The App is intended for use by its adult account holder and is not designed for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This notice will be updated if the App’s features, service providers, or data practices materially change. The effective date at the top will be changed when an update is published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests to delete information held by the App, use the private contact option available on the operator’s GitHub profile: github.com/xnnrmv. Do not post personal health information in public issues or discussions.
